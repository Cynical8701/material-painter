PBR texture sets from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), released under CC0
(public domain). Each folder is named after its Poly Haven asset id, e.g.
https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_02. Maps are 2K: color (diffuse),
rough, height, and metal.png (metalness) where the material is bare metal.
height.jpg combines the asset's displacement map with fine detail
integrated from its normal map.
